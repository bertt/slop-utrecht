# Slop Utrecht

A simulation of the inner city of Utrecht built with [three.js](https://threejs.org/), WebGPURenderer, and TSL. Explore the Domtoren, Oudegracht and Centraal Station on foot, by car, by bicycle or cargo bike, or by helicopter.

[Try it live](https://bertt.github.io/slop-utrecht/)

Based on [Slop New York](https://github.com/mrdoob/slop-newyork) by mrdoob.

## Make it your own

Fork it, remix it, improve it, break it. Make games, animations, films, experiments, or something completely different. Use it as a starting point and take it wherever you want. Have fun!

## Ideas

- Integrate more data from the [Utrecht Portolan catalog](https://browser.portolan-sdi.org/#/external/utrecht.blob.core.windows.net/catalog/catalog.json), such as tree heights and other open city layers.
- Better textures from aerial photos (luchtfoto's) for ground, roofs and water.
- Send a PR. Build the city!

Explore other cities and community remixes in [Slop Cities](https://github.com/zz85/slop-cities).
